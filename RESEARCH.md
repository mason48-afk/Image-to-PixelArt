## Product and FAQ targets

The converter targets relevant intent clusters, without stuffing unrelated controller keywords:

- Core conversion: image to pixel art, photo to pixel art, 32×32 and 64×64 conversion.
- Output control: transparent PNG pixel art, limited color palettes, dithering.
- Troubleshooting: blurry pixel-art export, crisp PNG enlargement, local processing and image privacy.

Features support these questions directly: adjustable columns, square-input guidance, preserved alpha, palette selection, optional ordered dithering, nearest-neighbor export and no image-upload server. Multilingual pages have unique titles, descriptions, self-canonical URLs and reciprocal hreflang links. The FAQ is visible, helpful content; no rich-result eligibility is promised.

`npm test` checks conversion invariants and static SEO correctness. It does not measure search volume, ranking difficulty, Google's indexing decisions or real-world accessibility/performance. Search Console data after launch should guide expansion. Advertising approval requires separate review of the final property; a working tool and ad space alone do not ensure acceptance.
