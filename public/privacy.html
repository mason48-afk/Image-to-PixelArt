Privacy & disclosure

Updated October 11, 2026. PixelPress is operated by Mason Payne.

Your images

Image decoding, resizing and color conversion happen in your browser. PixelPress does not send your image, its filename or the exported PNG to a processing server. Images remain in the page’s memory until replaced or the page is closed. Downloaded files are saved through your browser.

Local preferences

We save your selected language in browser local storage. You can clear it using your browser’s site-data controls. The converter works if storage is unavailable.

Hosting

Cloudflare delivers the website and may process network information such as IP address, request details and security logs. Local image processing does not mean that website visits are anonymous. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

This notice will be updated before activation. See Google’s partner-site information.
